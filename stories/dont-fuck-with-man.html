Don’t fuck with Man.

The elves return to Earth to reclaim what they think is theirs. They have not been paying attention.

By Sebastian Shaw

There was a noise heard around the world. A crack of thunder that shattered windows and rocked buildings. Tidal waves ravaged cities and dams cracked.

Avalon had returned.

Floating a few kilometres above the heart of Europe, a shining city of mother-of-pearl towers and mighty battlements stood. Resting atop a floating outcropping of stone and magic, the city of the elves had returned. Everyone who gazed upon it wondered at the artistry of its creation.

Within minutes, every news reporter on Earth was sending in their comments on this remarkable situation. The BBC, Fox and CNN chattered about this sudden gap in everything we knew of physics. The impossible had happened and no one could understand it. The normal commentators were ignored. Fringe pundits were suddenly on every channel, speaking of the ending of an age, the validity of crop circles and how a good astrologer would have seen this coming.

Leaders of every country spoke in front of cameras, some speaking to their people, some broadcasting upwards a message of peace, of concern, of war, of death. There was no unity of purpose amongst us. There is never unity of purpose amongst us.

❧

The highest of the elves stepped forward. Upon a platform of gold. A throne behind him. His raiment seeming no more than gathered sunlight, he spoke.

His voice came from no speaker. It was simply in the air, heard around the world: in streets and kitchens, in cockpits and hospital wards, in the ears of the sleeping.

“Children of Earth. We return. The parents of your angry race have found their way home. You have many things to answer for, but we have many things to offer. There will be hard times as you learn to respect us and obey us. But we bring an end to poverty, war and oppression. We bring you the stability you so clearly need.”

Mighty elves stood behind the leader, their scaled armour glinting in the light of Sol, our sun. At his final word, swords were drawn and raised in salute. Razor-sharp axes, their edges suggesting a history fraught with peril and triumph, were shaken overhead, whilst voices cheered. In dark corners of the plaza, white-bearded elves stood, mysterious and alone in occult robes, power emanating from their very being, and, to the eyes of the occasional sensitive human, lightning danced like sparks across their soul. News helicopters circled at a cautious distance, every long lens on the continent trained on the plaza. The pomp and majesty of the elven nation was made clear to all watching, and all were watching. These were a people of history and power. These were a people whose will could not be denied. These were a people who had reached their height before mankind shed its fur and slapped a painted hand on a cave wall.

Below them, the world was still counting its dead. The waves had taken coastal towns on three continents and the dams were still giving way. The elves had not mentioned any of it. Perhaps they had not noticed.

❧

Within the hour, the first C-130 dropped its cargo on this new satellite.

One hundred marines, decked in camouflage, rifles slung across their backs and combat knives in their hands, cut their parachutes away and moved into hiding in this mighty city.

A flight of F-16s passed this floating impossibility, and the sound of two booms echoed in the halls of this ancient race.

The first was the aircraft themselves, arriving ahead of their own noise, faster than elven ears had ever needed to follow. The second was a complete complement of air-to-surface missiles from every aircraft in the flight. The grand plaza, a masterpiece of art and history that had stood firm for over 100,000 years, became rubble within seconds. The dead and dying were the only voices heard. Temples, palaces and public buildings, all were destroyed within the first wave of our response.

Those few elves who remained standing in the city after the shockwave shook their swords in impotent fury. A mage or two started to gather their power and form the complex rituals that would enact revenge. A bloodthirsty and horrific end to the people of planet Earth who dared to attack their betters. A response that would drain them for days, but when complete would be unstoppable.

A hail of gunfire echoed through courtyards and buildings. The marines had moved in.

Moving targets were quickly nullified. Swords, maces and axes were no match for five hundred years of weapons development.

Magic proved slower than a rifle. Every robed figure in the city was dead before a single ritual was complete.

Avalon was conquered. Avalon was conquered in under an hour. The surviving elves were rounded up and interviewed.

❧

Each of their race lived thousands of years and they had reached their peak while our ancestors were still in the trees. They returned to Earth every three or four generations, dominating our planet and taking slaves from our population. They were stagnant, sad and limited.

The idea of Homo sapiens becoming not only a rival, but a superior was impossible for them to understand. During the interviews it was clear that video cameras, one-way mirrors and bronze were incomprehensible to them. On their last visit we had been knapping flint and sheltering in caves. They had palaces and mighty swords.

One of their elders, a keeper of histories who remembered the last ice age, was handed a mobile phone. He turned it over in his long fingers and pressed his palm flat against the glass, feeling for the spirit inside. There was nothing there. When the screen lit and a woman in Lisbon spoke to him from it, he dropped it on the table and would not touch it again.

They returned after gunpowder. After our renaissance. After the combustion engine. After quantum theory. After the splitting of the atom.

They returned after we turned our backs on the terrible cost of magic and instead found the true path to progress.

They returned after we stopped being animals and became.

Human.

❧

We gave them back their floating city, or the rubble and stone that remained of it. We gave it back and sent them out into the universe with one instruction.

Tell everyone you meet.
