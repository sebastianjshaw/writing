Don’t fuck with Man.

The elves return to Earth to reclaim what they think is theirs. They have not been paying attention.

By Sebastian Shaw

There was a noise heard around the world. A crack of thunder that shattered windows and rocked buildings. Tidal waves ravaged cities and dams cracked.

Avalon had returned.

Floating high above the bright blue earth, a shining city of mother-of-pearl towers and mighty battlements stood. Resting atop a floating outcropping of stone and magic, the city of the elves had returned. No one who gazed upon it could not help but wonder about the artistry of its creation.

Within minutes, every news reporter on earth was sending in their comments on this remarkable situation. The BBC, Fox, CNN chattered with this sudden break in the scientific method. The impossible had happened and no one could understand it. The normal commentators were ignored. Fringe pundits were suddenly on every channel. Speaking of the ending of an age, the validity of crop circles and how a good astrologer would have seen this coming.

Leaders of every country spoke in front of cameras, some speaking to their people, some broadcasting upwards a message of peace, of concern, of war, of death. There was no unity of purpose amongst us. There is never unity of purpose amongst us.

❧

The highest of the elves stepped forward. Upon a platform of gold. A throne behind him. His raiment seeming no more than gathered sunlight, he spoke:

Every device on earth related his message.

Televisions portrayed his visage.

Radio broadcast his voice.

Streaming services suddenly were replaced by a calm and focused face, radiant in its confidence.

“Children of earth. We return. The parents of your angry race have found their way home. You have many things to answer for, but we have many things to offer. There will be hard times as you learn to respect us and obey us. But we bring an end to poverty, war and oppression. We bring you the stability you so clearly need.”

Mighty elves stood behind the leader, their scaled armour glinting in the light of Sol, our sun. At his final word, swords were drawn and raised in salute. Razor-sharp axes, their edges suggesting a history fraught with peril and triumph were shaken overhead, whilst voices cheered. In dark corners of the plaza, white-bearded elves stood, mysterious and alone in occult robes, power emanating from their very being, and, to the eyes of the occasional sensitive human, lightning danced like sparks across their soul. The pomp and majesty of the elven nation was made clear to all watching, and all were watching. These were a people of history and power. These were a people whose will could not be denied. These were a people who had reached their height before mankind shed its fur and slapped a painted hand on a cave wall.

❧

Before the cheer had faded from hearing, the first of the military shuttles dropped its cargo on this new satellite.

One hundred marines, decked in camouflage and with MP5s strapped to their backs, and combat knives in their hands, cut their parachutes away and moved into hiding in this mighty city.

A flight of F-16s passed this floating impossibility, the sound of two booms echoed in the halls of this ancient race.

The first, a boom of moving over 344 meters per second, faster than even their elven ears could comprehend, broke the silence. The second was a complete complement of air-to-surface missiles from every aircraft in the flight. The grand plaza, a masterpiece of art and history that had stood firm for over 100,000 years, became rubble within seconds. The dead and dying were the only voices heard. Temples, palaces and public buildings, all were destroyed within the first wave of our response.

Those few elves who remained standing in the city after the shockwave shook their swords in impotent fury. A mage or two started to gather their power and form the complex rituals that would enact revenge. A bloodthirsty and horrific end to the people on planet earth who dared to attack their betters. A response that would drain them for days, but when complete would be unstoppable.

A hail of gunfire echoed through courtyards and buildings. The marines had moved in.

Moving targets were quickly nullified. Swords, maces and axes were no match for five hundred years of weapons development.

Magic proved slower than an assault rifle.

Avalon was conquered. Avalon was conquered in under an hour. The surviving elves were rounded up and interviewed.

❧

Each of their race lived thousands of years and they had reached their peak during the Jurassic age. They returned to earth every three or four generations, dominating our planet and taking slaves from our population. They were stagnant, sad and limited.

The idea of Homo sapiens becoming not only a rival, but a superior was impossible for them to understand, indeed during the interviews, it was clear that video cameras, one-way mirrors and non-steel metals were incomprehensible to them. During their last visit we were using crude bronze tools and living in whatever shelter we could find. They had palaces and mighty swords.

They returned after our renaissance. After the splitting of the atom. After quantum theory. After gunpowder and the combustion engine.

They returned after we turned our backs on the lazy appeal of magic and instead found the true rules of the universe.

They returned after we stopped being animals and became.

Human.

❧

We gave them back their floating city, rubble and stone that remained. We gave it back and sent them out into the universe with one instruction. Tell everyone you meet.

“Don’t fuck with Man.”
